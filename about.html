About us

Practical hotel training built on daily experience

adarkenigma LLC was formed in 2022 by a hotel operator with more than twenty years of experience in front desk operations, night audit, staff supervision, and management.

Experience from daily hotel operations

The experience behind this business comes from daily hotel operations. It includes guest service, front desk work, night audit, cash reconciliation, PMS reporting, staff training, and shift management. It also includes supervising housekeeping teams, inspecting rooms, and coordinating room status between housekeeping and the front desk.

That experience is current. The founder continues to work as a night manager at a franchised limited-service hotel and remains familiar with brand standards, franchise reporting, loyalty programs, and the quality measures hotel owners monitor.

adarkenigma LLC is an independent business. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any hotel brand or franchisor.

Why training still matters when the software is good

A capable property management system can improve reservations, reporting, and financial accuracy. It still depends on employees who understand how to use it, communicate with other departments, and respond when a situation does not fit the usual procedure.

Software can calculate a total and produce a report, but it cannot fix an unclear handoff or teach an employee how to handle a difficult conversation. Our training focuses on the people and procedures that help a property use its existing technology more effectively.

How our hotel staff training works

  1. Observe first. We watch how the property actually runs before changing anything: the current workflow, the recurring complaints, and where information gets lost between shifts.
  2. Train on your systems. Staff practice on the property’s own property management system, its own paperwork, and its own standards, not a generic demonstration environment.
  3. Leave something behind. Every engagement ends with a written checklist or procedure the property keeps, plus recommendations for follow-up training.

Where we work

On-site services are primarily available between Richmond and Indianapolis, Indiana, including the communities along and near the Interstate 70 corridor. Limited remote consultation is also available for workflow reviews, training-plan development, report guidance, and checklist and procedure development. Travel outside the primary area may be considered depending on the project.

Where the business stands today

adarkenigma LLC is still developing its public client base. Most work so far has come through referrals and direct contacts. The business is focused on practical training and workflow support for smaller independent and franchised properties.
