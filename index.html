Make every check-in count

A reservation system handles the transaction. The employee at the desk handles the guest.

The front desk shapes a guest's first impression. Training covers greetings, active listening, check-in and check-out procedures, telephone etiquette, payment handling, and basic service recovery. The goal is to help employees solve routine problems confidently before they become complaints.

See front desk training

Smoother check-in,
cleaner night audits

Night audit brings together the day’s room activity, payments, and reports. Training covers required PMS reports, cash and card balancing, posting errors, unsettled folios, and clear notes for the morning shift. A consistent process makes mistakes easier to find and correct.
