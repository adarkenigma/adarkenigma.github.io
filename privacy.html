Privacy Policy

This policy explains how this website handles information.

Effective July 31, 2026.

Contact form information

The contact form asks for a name, email address, property name, topic, and message. The form runs entirely in the browser. It checks the entries for missing information and basic email format, but it does not transmit or store the information.

Email and other direct contact

If you contact the email address shown on this site outside the form, the message will be processed by the email providers involved. Information sent by email would be used only to review and respond to the inquiry.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not intentionally set cookies, run advertising trackers, or use a separate analytics service. The hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the site.

External links

This site includes links to outside organizations. Those websites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not apply to them.

Your choices

You may choose not to enter information in the form. Because the form does not submit or store entries, there is no form submission for adarkenigma LLC to retrieve or delete.

Contact
