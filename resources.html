Training programs and industry resources

This page provides sample program formats, a representative engagement outline, and common hotel terms. The material is written for independent and franchised limited-service properties, generally in the 40 to 120 room range.

What an engagement looks like

The outline below shows one way a housekeeping engagement could be organized. It is a representative example and is not tied to a specific hotel.

  1. Day one: observe. Meet with the owner or manager, talk through the recurring complaints, watch the current room-cleaning workflow, review carts, supplies, chemicals, and safety practices, and inspect several rooms to understand what the property’s standard actually is today.
  2. Training days: demonstrate and practice. Training covers correct room entry, guest privacy, choosing the right product for each surface, preventing cross-contamination, cart organization, cleaning sequence, safe lifting, and pacing. Staff then complete room inspections and receive feedback while they work.
  3. Final day: handoff. Staff complete rooms with little assistance, improvement areas are reviewed with management, and the property keeps a written checklist plus a recommended follow-up plan.

Hotel metrics your team should understand

These terms appear regularly in hotel reports and daily operations. Employees who understand them can read reports more accurately and communicate more clearly with managers.

Occupancy
The percentage of available rooms actually sold over a given period.
ADR: average daily rate
Average room revenue earned per occupied room. It answers “what did we sell rooms for?”
RevPAR: revenue per available room
Room revenue divided by every room available, sold or not. It combines occupancy and rate into one figure.
Competitive set
The nearby group of hotels a property is measured against, used to judge whether a change came from the property or from the whole market.
Market segment
The category a booking belongs to, such as retail, loyalty, or negotiated business. Segments explain who is filling the rooms.
Channel
Where a reservation came from, such as direct to the property, the brand website, a travel agency, or a third-party site.
Folio
The running account of charges and payments for one guest stay. Unsettled folios are a common night-audit finding.
Guest satisfaction score
Survey-based scoring of a stay, often broken out by service, cleanliness, rooms, and value.
