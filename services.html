What we do

Hotel staff training services

Every property has its own routines, paperwork, and expectations. We begin by observing the current workflow, then build training around the systems and standards already used at the hotel.

What good training does

Consistent greetings

A clear greeting standard for every shift.

Accurate overnight reporting

A repeatable process for balancing reports and finding errors.

Reliable room standards

More consistent room standards from one shift to the next.

Clear department handoffs

Housekeeping and the front desk working from the same room-status information.

Training area and intended outcome

Each training area is connected to a practical operating problem. The table below shows the intended focus of each program.

Training areas, the common problem each addresses, and the intended outcome
Training area Common problem Intended outcome
Front deskInconsistent guest greetingsMore professional, repeatable guest interactions
Night auditRepeated reporting and posting errorsMore accurate and better organized overnight reports
HousekeepingInconsistent cleaning proceduresMore consistent room standards between staff
ManagementPoor communication between departmentsClearer expectations and cleaner shift handoffs

Note: These are intended outcomes, not measured results from a specific client property. Results will vary by hotel, staff experience, and follow-up from management.

Housekeeping training

Housekeeping training focuses on consistent room standards and safe work practices. Staff learn a clear cleaning sequence, correct product use, guest privacy procedures, and how to report maintenance concerns.

  • Guest privacy and correct room-entry procedure
  • Choosing the right product for each surface, and preventing cross-contamination
  • Safe chemical handling and protective equipment
  • Efficient cart setup and cleaning sequence
  • Room inspection standards and maintenance reporting
  • Safe lifting, pacing, and hydration

Front desk and desk clerk training

Front desk training covers the daily tasks employees use most often, along with the communication skills needed when a guest has a question or complaint.

  • Greeting, active listening, and professional appearance
  • Check-in and check-out on your property management system
  • Telephone etiquette and reservation questions
  • Cash drawer and payment procedures
  • Complaint handling and first-level service recovery
  • Room-status communication and shift handoff

Night auditor training

Night auditors often work with limited supervision, so the audit process needs to be clear and repeatable. Training emphasizes accurate reports, balanced accounts, and useful notes for the morning team.

  • Your property’s specific night-audit sequence
  • Running and reading the required reports
  • Balancing cash, card activity, and room revenue
  • Finding posting errors and resolving unsettled folios
  • Reviewing no-shows, cancellations, and room status
  • Overnight incident documentation and morning handoff

Back office and night-audit workflow

Some problems come from unclear handoffs rather than a lack of effort. This service helps owners and managers document procedures and improve communication between departments.

  • Observation of your current workflow end to end
  • Practical checklists and written standard procedures
  • A repeatable onboarding structure for new hires
  • Communication between front desk, housekeeping, and management
  • Expectations documented so they can be taught, not guessed
  • Follow-up training plans
